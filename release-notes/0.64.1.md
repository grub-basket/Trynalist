# 0.64.1

Follow-up to 0.64.0.

## Trash

An enhancement over Dynalist, which has no trash. Deleting an item (or a
selection, or completed items) now moves it — with its whole subtree — into a
dated group under a `_trash` folder instead of removing it. Nothing is lost until
you say so.

- **Open trash** — a panel lists each deletion with **Restore** (puts the items
  back in their document, structure intact) and **Empty**. Reachable from a Trash
  button in the Documents panel header and an "Open trash" action on the mobile
  toolbar.
- **Empty trash** routes through Obsidian's own trash, honouring your Obsidian
  setting (system trash, vault `.trash`, or permanent).
- Only real deletes go to the trash; internal moves keep working as before.
- A link to a trashed item is left dangling (and the link audit will show it)
  rather than being quietly repointed into the trash.

## Import

- The import and update **completion notices now stay on screen** until you
  dismiss them — a long run often finishes while you're away, and the summary
  should still be there when you get back.
