# 0.37.0

## Deleting a line works while text is selected

`Mod+D` and `Mod+Shift+K` did nothing whenever any text was highlighted inside
the item. They shared a check with copy and cut, which deliberately step aside
in that situation so the ordinary copy can happen — but deleting a line has no
such conflict. Both now work whatever is highlighted. Copy and cut still yield.

## Select-all widens one step at a time

`Mod+A` on an item jumped straight from that item to the entire document,
skipping the step most people want: the item together with its children. It now
climbs one ancestor at a time, taking each parent's subtree along the way.

## Navigation

The back and forward buttons moved to the left of the breadcrumb and now match
the size of the view buttons beside them.

**Hold either button** — or right-click it — to see a list of where it would
take you, and jump several steps at once.

Starting a search now counts as a step, so Back leaves a search rather than
Escape being the only way out.
