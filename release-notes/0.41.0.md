# 0.41.0

## Dragging a selection moves all of it

Dragging a row that was part of a multi-item selection moved only that row and
quietly discarded the selection. It now carries the whole selection, keeps the
items in their original order, and refuses a drop into their own subtree.

## Move to… stops dead-ending

- Searching now matches **document names** as well as item text, because
  "move to *that document*" is what people actually look for.
- Every other document offers a **top level** destination, so an empty document —
  or one whose items you cannot recall — is somewhere you can move things.
- Moved items land at the **end** of the destination.

## The bookmark star

A star sits in the breadcrumb bar, always visible, filled only when the view you
are looking at is bookmarked — document, zoom and search together. Bookmark a
document, zoom into a child, and it correctly offers to bookmark that view
rather than to un-bookmark the document.

Right-click it to rename the bookmark or jump to the bookmarks pane. Bookmark
names live in settings and never touch the document itself. An item's menu can
also bookmark that item directly.

## Notifications

The reload notice now has a proper layout — an accent for its kind, the message,
and its action button — and reads correctly in both light and dark themes.
